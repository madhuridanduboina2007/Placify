✨ About Placify

Connecting Students With Opportunities

Placify is a college placement management system designed to make the placement journey simple, organized, and accessible for students, administrators, and companies.

🎓

For Students

Students can create their profile, explore available jobs, check eligibility, apply for suitable opportunities, and track their applications.

🏢

For Companies

Companies can be registered in the placement system and their job opportunities can be managed efficiently.

⚙️

For Administrators

Administrators can manage students, companies, jobs, applications, and other placement-related information from one centralized system.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Campus Placements Simple & Organized

Placify brings important placement activities together in one platform. Instead of managing information across different systems, students and administrators can access the information they need from a single place.
